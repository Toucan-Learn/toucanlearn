About Toucan Learn

A different approach to tutoring

We believe effective learning starts with understanding the learner, rather than simply giving them more work. Our tutors look beyond worksheets and grades to identify confidence, motivation, learning gaps and any barriers that may be making learning more difficult.

We support learners across KS1 to KS4, including children who are neurodivergent, have additional educational needs, feel anxious about learning or have lost confidence in the classroom.

How we work

Support shaped around the learner

01

We understand

Every learner begins with a free diagnostic session. This helps us understand their needs, strengths, confidence, interests and current barriers.

02

We personalise

Sessions are adapted around the learner. We adjust the teaching style, pace, structure and activities instead of relying on one fixed programme.

03

We build confidence

Our aim is not only academic progress. We also help learners become more confident, independent and willing to engage with challenges.
