About Toucan Learn

A different approach to tutoring

We believe effective learning starts with understanding the learner, rather than simply giving them more work. Our tutors look beyond grades and worksheets to identify confidence, motivation, learning gaps and any barriers that may be making learning more difficult.

We support learners across KS1 to KS4, including children who are neurodivergent, have additional educational needs, feel anxious about learning or have lost confidence in the classroom.

01

We understand

Every learner begins with a free diagnostic session so we can understand their needs, strengths, interests and current barriers.

02

We personalise

Sessions are built around the learner. We adapt the teaching style, pace and activities instead of relying on a single fixed programme.

03

We build confidence

Our aim is not only academic progress. We also help learners become more confident, independent and willing to engage with challenges.
